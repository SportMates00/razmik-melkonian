Teaching

Piano Teaching

Thoughtful, individual teaching for students of all ages and levels, in London and online.

01 — Approach
Technique, imagination and musicianship developed together.

Razmik is a pianist, prize-winning accompanist and experienced piano teacher. He is a graduate of the Royal Academy of Music and Royal Birmingham Conservatoire.

His teaching encourages students to explore music deeply and develop creativity and individuality through reading, writing and improvisation, alongside performance skills.

All ages & levels

Lessons shaped around each student’s experience and goals.

Exams

Performance and theory preparation, including ABRSM pathways.

Core skills

Technique, sight-reading, aural awareness and musicianship.

Creativity

Improvisation, composition and exploratory learning.
