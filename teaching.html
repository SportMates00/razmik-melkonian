Teaching

Piano Teaching

Teaching for students of all ages and levels, in London and online.

Razmik is a pianist, prize-winning accompanist and experienced piano teacher. He is a graduate of The Royal Academy of Music and Royal Birmingham Conservatoire.

As a piano teacher with a wide range of interests across genres and a deep, ongoing exploration of music, he inspires his students to explore its depths and reveal the secrets of the art form. He encourages them to foster their creativity and individuality by integrating reading, writing and improvisation - skills essential not only for developing a well-rounded musician, but also for fostering a profound understanding and appreciation of music.

  • All levels, all ages
  • Exam preparation (performance and theory)
  • Individual approach
  • Technique and aural skills
  • Improvisation
